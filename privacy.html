My Budget AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

My Budget AI helps you plan a monthly budget. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. The short version: we keep only what you type into the app, we use it only to run the app for you, and we never sell it or show you ads.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider, so no one can read it.
  • Your budgets: the numbers and names you enter, such as income, bills, savings and expense names, plus the results the app calculates and when each budget was saved.
  • Your settings: choices like how suggested values work.
  • Sharing: if you share your budgets, the email address of each person you share with.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, bank logins or account numbers. Bank or card CSV files you import are read on your own device; only the totals you choose to apply are saved in your budget. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, save and show your budgets, send account emails you ask for (confirming your email, resetting your password), and let the people you choose see your budgets. We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see your budgets

  • You, when signed in.
  • People you share with. If you add someone's email under "Share with Spouse", they can view and edit all of your budgets from their own account (but not delete them). You can stop sharing at any time, and they can remove themselves.
  • Our service providers, only to store and deliver the app (see below).

The database enforces these rules, so one person's budgets can't be read by anyone else.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores your account and budgets and sends account emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library the app needs to sign you in.

Like any website, these providers can see technical details such as your IP address and browser type when your device connects to them. Mortgage rates shown in the app come from Freddie Mac's public data and are loaded with the app, so looking them up doesn't send anything about you.

Information stored on your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in so you stay signed in, and the app keeps a copy of your budgets on your device so you can view them with no internet connection. Signing out removes that copy. When you import a bank or card CSV, the app remembers on your device which category you picked for each merchant, so the next import sorts them the same way. If you use the mortgage calculator's "Use in my budget" button, its numbers are kept on your device for up to a day so they can be added to a budget.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your account and budgets until you delete them.
  • Delete a single budget with the ✕ on it.
  • Delete your whole account with Delete Account at the top of the app. This permanently removes your account, all of your budgets and your sharing settings right away. It can't be undone.
  • Download a copy of all your budgets first with ⚙️ Settings → Download my data. The file stays on your device, and Restore from a backup adds its budgets back to an account later.

Security

Information travels between your device and our providers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and each person can only reach their own budgets and those shared with them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

My Budget AI is meant for adults and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: TheMarkFaul@gmail.com

You can also download or delete your data yourself at any time from the app: ⚙️ Settings → Download my data, and Delete Account at the top of the app.
