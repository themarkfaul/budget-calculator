🧾 Self-Escrow

🧾 Self-Escrow Plan

A self-escrow is a separate checking account you set up and fund yourself to pay your fixed bills. The rest of your pay goes into a spending account for day-to-day money. Check the bills to pay from your self-escrow; for bills paid once or twice a year, pick when they're due.

This isn't the escrow account your mortgage company manages. If your lender already pays your property tax or homeowners insurance from your mortgage payment, uncheck those bills here so they aren't counted twice.
